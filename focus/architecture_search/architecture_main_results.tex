Across 30 independent architecture proposals from input/output pairs, 28 were executable and 21 matched every original extrapolation output. All three proposals were exact for square, hyperbolic tangent, addition, subtraction, running sum, Fibonacci and triangular numbers. The sine proposals had median outer RMSE $7.79\times10^{-16}$; sorting errors were about $5.2\times10^{-16}$. Positive self-power had one executable proposal with RMSE $1.68\times10^4$ and two invalid proposals. Astra chose feedforward arithmetic or activation graphs and recurrent networks; no proposal chose convolution or attention. Square used one allowed multiplication, zero stored coefficients and 16 bytes of live activation storage. Its graph specification still occupied 109 serialized bytes. Operator choice explains this reduction relative to a ReLU-only network. The trials establish observed implementations and resource costs, with no global architecture optimum claimed.
