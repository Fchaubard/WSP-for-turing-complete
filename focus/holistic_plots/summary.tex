We average each task's raw extrapolation RMSE across its network sizes and three starts, then sum those ten task values. This gives every task equal weight in the sum. Iterative WSP scores $2.026\times10^9$ in each information condition. The examples-only one-shot score is $1.058\times10^{12}$, about 522 times larger. Direct L-BFGS from Xavier scores $7.974\times10^{11}$, and trust-region least squares scores $8.848\times10^{11}$. These values reflect the units and scale of the targets. Fibonacci contributes 99.8\% of the examples-only one-shot total, while positive self-power contributes 78.1\% of its iterative total. Across the same settings the median scaled RMSE rises after iteration. The raw sum and the scaled median therefore answer different aggregation questions.
