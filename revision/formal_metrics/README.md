# Appendix metric reproduction

Run rmse.py from this directory with NumPy in the original numerical environment. It replays all 945 archived matched-optimizer vectors and verifies their original exact fractions independently for train, central (or early sequence) and outer sets. optimizer_rmse.csv contains all 2,835 raw-RMSE measurements, with every output coordinate in the denominator. The reconstruction rescales residuals by the maximum before squaring when necessary to avoid overflow. The result is in the target's original units. Training early/outer splits for sequences are single coupled trajectories.

recurrent_rmse.csv gives raw RMSE, normalized RMSE and whole-stream exact fractions from both budgets, all widths and models. No checkpoint was available for a recurrent WSP sorter or a width-one seeded recurrence. Their manuscript cells display a dash. scaled_cell.py compares the two-coefficient WSP accumulator against the five supervised fits on exactly the same data and produces scaled_cell.csv. The signed-index and signed self-power comparisons are in ../other_metrics/secondary.csv.

The script also regenerates the three appendix table fragments in paper/. Shallow-sweep RMSEs pool both equally sized shell sides before taking their square root, then take the median of the three trained starts. Positive self-power uses the positive side. For further tables, writer/task_probe.py score recalculates exact fractions and raw RMSE in the original nine-response pilot. extension/convex/run.py writes raw RMSE and exact fractions next to scaled errors across the frozen-feature controls. extension/audit/lattice.py computes RMSE for the grid witnesses and their transfer sets.
