# Exact matches and raw root mean squared error

Start with **output/iterative-wsp-endpoints.csv** for individual one-shot and iterative weight-space programming (WSP) endpoints and both separate gradient-descent checkpoints. The `dev` identifier means untouched central evaluation or unseen integer indices 17–32; `feedback_dev` means the development pool consulted during revision; `legacy_early_9_32` includes indices used for feedback. Fresh confirmation draws are named separately and apply only to sampled-input tasks. Exact counts require every coordinate of a stream or sorted list to match; raw RMSE pools coordinates in the target's units. **output/iterative-wsp-by-size.csv** gives medians for each architecture, and **output/iterative-wsp-rounds.csv** gives the fixed-cohort progression through the three opportunities to revise. For candidate-level records see **focus/iterative_eval/stage_scores.csv**; retrospective test scores for rejected proposals were unavailable to selection.

Use output/nonconvex-endpoints.csv for the original one-shot common-objective experiment. It names every task, architecture, parameter count, method and split, with training loss and raw error at the same returned weights. focus/endpoints contains paired comparisons, focus/nonconvex contains the eligibility audit, focus/objective and focus/arithmetic contain the fresh WSP results, and focus/refinement contains the secondary least-squares continuation from WSP.

For the gradient-descent and limited-memory comparisons, use output/gradient-descent-and-lbfgs.csv. It joins all ten endpoint choices, including the initial Xavier array and the two WSP conditions, on the same three data splits. The coefficient audit under focus/coefficients identifies the raw model reply and the algebraic symmetry of each returned array.

For the historical extrapolation-first comparison, use output/ten-task-exact-and-rmse.csv. Its 1,476 rows cover the ten-task comparison at all three sizes and three initializations. Its four methods are weight-space programming (WSP) with the formula and examples, WSP with examples only, backpropagation with examples only, and the initial Xavier weights. The displayed manuscript table uses the outer split and takes the median across the three starts at its specified size. An exact_fraction of 1 means every example matched. Raw RMSE averages squared errors over scalar outputs within a run in that target’s original units before taking the square root. For sorted lists and running sums the exact fraction counts whole examples. For Fibonacci and triangular sequences it counts indices.

The two quoted WSP information conditions both received the example labels, architecture, initial coefficients and deployment envelope. Only “Formula and examples” received the stated rule. “Outer” names the primary held-out split for that task. “Observed” names the examples supplied for fitting. For the signed scalar tasks, “left” and “right” give each outer interval separately. “Inner” draws inputs in the central range. For integer recurrences, “fit_indices” and “early” separate fitted and early unseen indices. The flexible-size stream controls include separate length and joint tests.

Each remaining dataset follows its originating evaluation. The three study READMEs describe the exact-match unit and sampling scheme. Preserve split, task and reference when comparing errors.

- revision/formal_metrics contains optimizer checkpoint and recurrent neural network replays. For the 945 optimizer fits, one row per split distinguishes training, development and held-out errors.
- revision/numeric_metrics contains the elementary-function circuits, free-topology model responses, their feedback revisions, square proofs and register-machine experiments. Invalid responses are labeled. Some wide-exponent comparisons have nonfinite errors.
- revision/other_metrics contains individual streams, integer-index experiments, ablations and the matched sparse recurrent BP control. Its large frontier sweeps include many correlated measurements.
- scalar contains per-size results for the original scalar controls. extension/convex/scores.csv contains the 2,016 fixed-hidden-feature configurations.

The raw pilot observations are in writer/task_scores.json. The finite square-grid witnesses and their off-grid errors are in extension/audit/lattice.json. output/optimizer-runs.csv joins all 945 optimizer checkpoints with their losses, exact rates and replayed errors.

The companion optimizer report, proof manuscript, executable coefficients, generation scripts, outputs and prompts are in the research bundle.
