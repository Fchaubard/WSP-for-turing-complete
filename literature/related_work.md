# Public record and closest precedents

Sources checked through November 5, 2026 (Pacific time). The X status IDs below were recovered from indexed posts or an independent author's direct link. The original X page was access-limited. Numerical claims below come from the respective authors' reports and were not re-run as part of this literature search.

## The public exchange

- On November 2, Rikiya Takehi announced new experiments by Takehi, Jayden Teoh, Alex Zhang and Omar Khattab. Their most relevant experiment is number 5. Given a small all-zero tanh MLP, its interfaces, game rules and source, Astra specified weights for three MinAtar games in a single reply. The authors report beating their 100M-step PPO comparator on all three games. They also provide an interactive, Python-assisted variant for Go. The starting matrices in those synthesis experiments were zero. [Original X announcement](https://x.com/rikiyatakehi/status/2117313742891327994) · [Indexed rendering](https://x.noodl3.net/rikiyatakehi/status/2117313742891327994) · [Authors' report](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/) · [Original logs at commit 4d09ba6](https://github.com/RikiyaT/lm_understand-logs/tree/4d09ba6d6a7e891dd8063974dfe81b3d78e3494f)
- The widely shared Qwen result concerned a pretrained model with repetitive responses. The authors report MATH-500 rising from 7.9% to 48.1% under the same decoding settings, after a budget of 50 batched practice probes. On a separate Gemma 3 270M experiment they found little change. An independent byte-level audit by Carlos Toledo counted 131,072 bfloat16 entries addressed and 130,188 actually changed. His separate unedited non-thinking run reached 44.0% under different decoding settings. These comparisons bear on interpretation of the mechanism. [Authors' experiments](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/) · [Independent audit and controls](https://carlostoledo.co/reports/weight-space-programming.html)
- The next wave included one-shot policy-writing comparisons by Wenhao Chai and Xingyu Dang. The posts establish what those authors tested. Sean Goedecke also reconstructed an interpretable first-layer visualization and challenged the claim of superhuman performance. These discussions supply context for baseline choices. [Chai's post](https://x.com/wenhaocha1/status/2117383529273405747) · [Dang's post](https://x.com/xingyudang/status/2117428789068386630) · [Goedecke's November 3 analysis](https://www.seangoedecke.com/llms-are-not-superhuman-at-reading-and-writing-neural-network-weights/)

## Most important overlapping work

- On November 2, Chandan Singh published an especially close algorithmic study. A Claude coding agent wrote 959 weight programs for 13 tasks. On ten discrete algorithmic tasks, including bounded-digit addition, multiplication and sorting, at least one proposal for each task achieved exact output match on 200 tested inputs within three tries. The agent repeatedly evaluated and simplified programs, and could change the transformer architecture. This establishes prior work on both algorithmic weight writing and empirical parameter minimization. Their tokenized finite-domain tests and smallest-found sizes leave open per-function lower bounds and numerical precision on real-valued tests. [Report](https://csinva.io/weight-space-programming-autoresearch/analyze/toy_examples/report.html) · [Code and run logs](https://github.com/csinva/weight-space-programming-autoresearch)
- Cory Trimm's concurrent public project asks several models to compile benefit formulas into small networks, tests agreement with the formulas, compares with trained controls and considers the effect of rounding steps. This is direct precedent for formula-to-weight synthesis on numerical targets, including piecewise-linear functions. [Repository snapshot as of November 4](https://github.com/ctrimm/weight-space-programming/tree/eb1c4eeb6184e6ee0bef6c5aa91a9517e50b904e)
- Humza and Omar Nusrat studied a different use, blind auditing and repair of small clinical protocol networks with deliberately introduced flaws. Their November 3 preprint reports incomplete detection, illustrating why a successful repair is not a certificate of correctness. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05341)

## Constructive models, extrapolation and exactness

- Siegelmann compiled a formal neural language into analog recurrent networks in 1994. Siegelmann and Sontag established Turing-computability results under a specified computational model. Bounded-state, fixed-precision arithmetic and finite approximation on a compact interval pose different mathematical questions. [Neural Programming Language](https://cdn.aaai.org/AAAI/1994/AAAI94-134.pdf) · [On the Computational Power of Neural Nets](https://doi.org/10.1006/jcss.1995.1013)
- Tracr compiles programs such as sorting into decoder-only transformers with known mechanisms. Velez-Ginorio et al.'s Cajal compilers target linear neurons and linear recurrent neurons, with correctness proofs. These are prior art for making circuits by construction, independent of the author of the formulas. [Tracr](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05062) · [Compiling to Linear Neurons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.13769) · [Compiling to Recurrent Neurons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14953)
- Cybenko shows uniform approximation to continuous functions on the unit cube with a suitable sigmoidal activation. Yarotsky supplies constructive ReLU square approximation and depth/size dependence. A finite one-dimensional ReLU network has finitely many affine pieces, so its curvature rules out equality with a quadratic across any open interval. VC/pseudodimension rates count shattering capacity over a function class; a particular target's width or edit threshold calls for an architecture-specific argument. [Cybenko](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02551274) · [Yarotsky](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.01145) · [Bartlett et al.](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v20/17-612.html)
- Neural Arithmetic Logic Units and Neural Arithmetic Units address extrapolation using arithmetic inductive biases and gradient training. Xu et al. analyze the tendency toward linear extrapolation along rays in a specified two-layer ReLU neural-tangent-kernel setting and demonstrate the role of task alignment. CLRS includes multiple sorting tasks and benchmarks generalization by algorithmic input size. These works motivate matching the architecture, information and numerical criterion when interpreting training comparisons. [Trask et al.](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2018/hash/0e64a7b00c83e3d22ce6b3acf2c582b6-Abstract.html) · [Madsen and Johansen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.05016) · [Xu et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.11848) · [CLRS](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/velickovic22a.html)
- The recurrent-width argument has classical precedents in minimal realization from Markov parameters. The technique of bounding coordinate-edit distance with an algebraic dimension count parallels work on matrix rigidity, whose target set is rank-constrained matrices. The retained specific contribution would be the dimension of the prefix-sum solution set and the edit budget for this fixed recurrent parameterization. [Ho and Kalman](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19670049337) · [Kumar et al.](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.FSTTCS.2009.2327)
